04 · The core three, proportion, the heat scale, contrast and the Salon Black theme.

Colour

The core three

CHI is a red, black and white brand. These three colours come straight from the logo artwork and do not change between themes.

Proportion

Red is powerful because there is not much of it. As a guide for any layout, pack or screen:

Share Colour Role
55% White and surface-raised Ground. Lets hair and product stand out.
25% chi-black / ink Type, tool bodies, the Salon Black theme.
15% chi-red / brand Logo, one headline panel, one action.
5% silk or the heat scale Story accents. One accent family per piece.

Campaign panels and packaging may invert the first and third rows, making red the ground. When they do, everything else on the panel is white. See ColorProportion below.

Neutral greys lean slightly warm so they sit comfortably beside red and beside skin and hair in photography.

The heat scale

Temperature is the one place CHI uses a colour sequence. heat-low, heat-mid and heat-high run from amber to orange to CHI Red, and each step is darker than the one before, so the order still reads in greyscale.

  • Use the heat colours only for temperature settings and heat-protection content.
  • Every band carries a temperature label in both °F and °C. The colour never stands alone.
  • Text on heat-low and heat-mid is on-heat; text on heat-high is on-brand.

Text and contrast

Every pairing below meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes.

Text On Light Salon Black
ink surface 19.2:1 19.2:1
ink-muted surface / surface-raised 7.1 / 6.5 8.8 / 8.0
brand-text surface / surface-raised 6.0 / 5.5 7.6 / 6.9
on-brand brand 5.0:1 5.0:1
on-brand brand-strong 7.0:1 7.0:1
on-silk silk 14.6:1 14.6:1
  • White on chi-red is 4.99:1. It passes for all text sizes, with little to spare, so never lighten the red or use a tint behind white text.
  • chi-red on chi-black is 3.8:1. That is enough for the logo and for headlines at 24px and above, and not enough for body copy. Red text on dark grounds uses brand-text.
  • Never put chi-red text on silk, or on the heat colours.

The Salon Black theme

The dark theme is not an inversion. It is the brand's black: tool bodies, backstage, the professional range. surface becomes chi-black, ink becomes white, and brand stays exactly chi-red. Use it for tools, professional and education content, and evening or event campaigns.

Status colours

success, caution and error exist for interfaces only. Because error shares the brand red, every error message carries an icon and says what went wrong in words. success is a blue-leaning green so it is never confused with the red by hue alone.

Brand roles

  • brand

    Fill for the one primary action on a screen, key headlines blocks and campaign panels. Carries on-brand text. Never use it as a text colour on dark grounds; use brand-text.

  • on-brand

    Text and icons on brand and brand-strong fills (4.99:1 and 7.0:1).

  • brand-strong

    Hover and pressed state of brand fills. Carries on-brand text.

  • brand-text

    Red used as text or as a link: on surface, surface-raised, surface-sunken and brand-tint. At least 4.99:1 on each in both themes.

  • brand-tint

    Quiet red wash behind promotional notes and selected rows. Carries ink or brand-text.

Surfaces and ink

  • surface

    Page and packaging ground.

  • surface-raised

    Ceramic grey. Cards, product tiles, alternate page bands.

  • surface-sunken

    Image placeholders, input wells, table headers.

  • ink

    Headlines and body copy on surface, surface-raised, surface-sunken and brand-tint.

  • ink-muted

    Captions, legal lines and secondary copy on surface, surface-raised and surface-sunken (5.9:1 or better).

  • line

    Decorative hairlines and dividers that carry no meaning on their own.

  • line-strong

    Borders of inputs and other controls on surface and surface-raised (3.4:1 or better in both themes).

  • focus

    Solid 2px focus ring, offset 2px, on every surface. On brand fills the ring sits outside the fill on the ground.

Silk and heat

  • silk

    Warm champagne for treatment, oil and care stories. A panel colour only, never text. Carries on-silk.

  • on-silk

    Text on silk panels (14.6:1).

  • heat-low

    Lowest band of the heat scale on styling tools and heat-protection content. Always paired with a temperature label. Carries on-heat.

  • heat-mid

    Middle band of the heat scale. Always paired with a temperature label. Carries on-heat.

  • heat-high

    Top band of the heat scale. Always paired with a temperature label. Carries on-brand.

  • on-heat

    Text on heat-low and heat-mid (10.4:1 and 6.8:1).

Status

  • success

    Confirmation text and icons on surface and surface-raised. Always with a word or icon, never colour alone.

  • caution

    Warning text and icons on surface and surface-raised. Always with a word or icon.

  • error

    Error text and icons on surface and surface-raised. Shares the brand red, so every error also carries an icon and the word that says what went wrong.
