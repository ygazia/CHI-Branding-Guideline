Brand guidelines · Draft 1.0

Heavy and light.
Heat and care.

CHI is a professional haircare and styling-tool brand from Farouk Systems, founded in Houston in 1986 by hairdresser Farouk Shami. The brand looks the way its wordmark looks: three heavy red letters, a light black descriptor, a white ground. Everything in these guidelines extends that contrast of heavy and light, red and black, heat and care.

The ten rules

  1. Use the supplied logo artwork. Never retype, redraw, stretch or recolour it.
  2. Keep clear space of one "I" width on every side of the logo. Nothing enters it.
  3. The wordmark is chi-red on white, or white on chi-red or chi-black. No other colours.
  4. Keep the ® on the logo at every size.
  5. Red is spent, not spread. brand covers about 15% of a layout; white and surface-raised carry the rest.
  6. Set everything in Archivo. Headlines are heavy (display-xl, display-l, headline); range names are light capitals (descriptor-l, descriptor-s); copy is regular (body).
  7. Corners are square: radius-none on buttons, cards, panels and photos.
  8. Hair is the hero of every photograph. Show real shine, real texture, real movement.
  9. Write the way a great stylist talks: specific, encouraging, no jargon without a reason.
  10. Every claim is true for the product it sits beside, and every temperature is given in both °F and °C.

Colour, in brief

  • Text is ink on surface, surface-raised or surface-sunken. Secondary text is ink-muted.
  • One brand button per screen, with on-brand text. Its hover is brand-strong.
  • Red as text or a link is brand-text, never chi-red directly, so it stays legible in the Salon Black theme.
  • silk panels belong to treatment, oil and care stories. heat-low, heat-mid and heat-high belong to temperature only.
  • success, caution and error always come with a word or icon.

Type, in brief

  • Headline: display-l or headline, sentence case, tight tracking, ends with a full stop when it is a statement.
  • Range or line name: descriptor-s or descriptor-l, capitals, wide tracking.
  • Copy: lead once, then body. Sizes and footnotes in caption; trademark and regulatory lines in legal.
  • Never set a headline in light weight or a descriptor in heavy weight. The contrast between the two is the brand.

Layout, in brief

  • Space with the scale: space-6 inside cards, space-8 under headlines, space-16 between sections.
  • Align to the left edge. Centre only the logo on packaging fronts and single-message posters.
  • Borders, not shadows: line for dividers, line-strong for controls. The system has no shadow tokens.
  • Focus is a solid 2px focus ring, offset 2px.
