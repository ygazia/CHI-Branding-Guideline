07 · Packaging, web, social, salon, stationery, advertising and partners.

Applications

Packaging

The pack front follows one order, top to bottom:

  1. CHI mark, centred, with 2X clear space.
  2. Line name as the supplied lockup descriptor, or in descriptor-l.
  3. Product name in title, sentence case.
  4. One benefit line in caption.
  5. Size in both units at the foot: 12 fl oz / 355 mL.
  • The master mark is the same proportion of the front panel across a line, so a shelf of CHI reads as one block.
  • Pack colour identifies the line; the wordmark stays chi-red on light packs and white on red or black packs.
  • Tools carry the mark in white on the black body and the temperature range in both °F and °C near the control.
  • Backs carry directions as three numbered steps, then ingredients and regulatory copy in legal.
  • Claims on pack match the claims approved for that formula in that market.

Website and e-commerce

  • White surface ground, ink type, one brand button per view. See Button.
  • Product tiles sit on surface-raised, image at 4:5, descriptor above the product name. See ProductCard.
  • Navigation and buttons are sentence case in body-strong.
  • The header carries the red mark at 96px wide on desktop and 72px on mobile, top left.
  • Sale and promotion use brand-tint rows and brand-text prices. No starbursts, no flashing.
  • Every interactive element has a visible focus ring and a 44px minimum touch target.
  • Tool pages show the heat range with HeatScale and link to a matching heat protectant.

Social media

  • Profile image: the white mark centred on chi-red, with clear space, cropped square. The platform applies its own circle.
  • Tiles: one message per tile. Either a full photograph with a flush colour panel for type, or a flat chi-red or chi-black tile with a display-l line. See SocialPost.
  • Grid rhythm: two photographs to every flat tile.
  • Video: opens on hair, not on a logo. The mark appears whole in the last second on white, red or black.
  • Captions: lead with the result, then the how-to, then the products. Tag stylists and credit their work.
  • User and stylist content: repost only with permission, credit in the first line, and do not add brand graphics over other people's photographs.

Salon and retail

  • Fascia and window: the red mark on white, or white on red, at one size across a site.
  • Shelf strips: chi-black with white descriptor-s line names; the mark at the left end only.
  • Backwash and station cards: one headline, three steps, one product group.
  • Education and events: Salon Black theme. Stage screens carry the white mark on black, bottom left.
  • Capes, aprons, tool bags: the white mark on black, embroidered or printed at no less than 40 mm wide.

Stationery and documents

  • Business card, 85 x 55 mm or 3.5 x 2 in: front white with the red mark top left and details in caption; back solid chi-red with the white mark centred. See Stationery.
  • Letterhead: red mark top left at 30 mm wide, address in legal at the foot, 25 mm margins.
  • Presentations: white slides, headline titles top left, the red mark bottom left at 24 mm. Section dividers are full chi-red with a white display-l line.
  • Email signature: name in bold, title and contact in regular, set in Arial at the recipient's default size. The mark as a 96px image, with alternative text "CHI". No quotes, banners or animated images.

Advertising

  • One photograph, one headline, one product group, one logo.
  • The headline sits in a flush panel or on a calm area of the photograph.
  • The mark sits bottom left or bottom right with at least 2X clear space.
  • Retailer or distributor details go in a white strip along the foot, in caption, separated by a line rule.

Distributors and partners

  • Use the supplied logo artwork exactly as supplied.
  • State the relationship in words, in descriptor-s: Authorised distributor, Official salon partner.
  • Do not build local logos, mascots or sub-brands that include the CHI name or mark.
  • Local-language campaigns translate the copy and keep the layout, colour and photography rules unchanged.
  • Send new packaging, advertising and signage for brand approval before production.
