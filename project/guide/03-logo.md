# Logo

## The master mark

The CHI logo is a wordmark: three heavy capital letters in `chi-red` with the registered symbol at the top right. The H and the I are built from plain rectangles, and that squareness sets the shape of the whole identity.

Always use the artwork in **Logos**. The live references are **LogoVersions**, **LogoClearSpace** and **LogoMisuse** in Components.

## Versions

| Artwork | Use on | File |
|---|---|---|
| Mark, red | White and `surface-raised`. The first choice. | `chi-mark-red.svg` |
| Mark, white | `chi-red`, `chi-black`, and dark photography. | `chi-mark-white.svg` |
| Mark, black | One-colour print only: newsprint, engraving, fax-grade documents. | `chi-mark-black.svg` |
| Line lockup, colour | White and `surface-raised`. | `chi-infra-lockup-color.svg` |
| Line lockup, reversed | `chi-black` and dark photography. Red mark, white descriptor. | `chi-infra-lockup-reversed.svg` |
| Line lockup, black or white | One-colour print, or white on `chi-red`. | `chi-infra-lockup-black.svg`, `chi-infra-lockup-white.svg` |

Choose in this order: red on white, white on red, white on black. Use the black version only when colour is not available.

## Line lockups

A line lockup places the line name under the mark in light capitals, in `chi-black`, with its own ®. CHI Infra is the reference lockup.

- The descriptor's capital height is one third of the wordmark's.
- The gap between the mark and the descriptor is half an "I" width.
- Lockups are supplied as artwork. Never build one by typing a line name under the mark.
- When a line has no supplied lockup, use the master mark and set the line name separately in `descriptor-l`, outside the clear space.

## Clear space

The unit is **X = the width of the letter I**, which is one eighth of the mark's width. Keep at least 1X clear on all four sides of the mark or lockup. On packaging fronts and posters, aim for 2X.

## Minimum size

| Artwork | Print | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| Master mark | 12 mm wide | 60 px wide |
| Line lockup | 20 mm wide | 96 px wide |

Below these sizes the ® and the descriptor fill in. If the space is smaller, use the master mark alone.

## Placement

- Top left or bottom left on layouts with other content.
- Centred on packaging fronts, signage and single-message posters.
- One logo per surface. Do not repeat it as a pattern.
- On photography, place it over a calm area of the image with at least 3:1 contrast against what is behind it.

## Backgrounds

| Ground | Artwork |
|---|---|
| White, `surface-raised`, `silk` | Red mark, or colour lockup |
| `chi-red` | White mark, or white lockup |
| `chi-black` | White mark, or reversed lockup |
| Light photography | Red mark, on a calm area |
| Dark photography | White mark |

Never place the red mark on `heat-mid`, `heat-low`, or any other red, orange or pink ground.

## Misuse

Do not:

- stretch, condense, rotate or skew the logo
- change its colours, or set the letters in different colours
- add outlines, shadows, glows, gradients or textures
- retype it in Archivo or any other typeface
- remove or resize the ®
- move, resize or recolour the descriptor of a lockup
- place it inside a shape that is not part of the layout grid, such as a circle or badge
- use it inside a sentence in place of the word CHI

## Co-branding

- Partner logos sit to the right of the CHI mark, separated by 2X and a thin `line` rule or a lowercase x in `descriptor-l`.
- Match the partner logo to the CHI mark by optical height, never larger.
- Distributors and salons use the line "Authorised distributor" or "Official salon partner" set in `descriptor-s` beneath the mark, outside the clear space. They do not combine the CHI mark with their own logo into a new lockup.
