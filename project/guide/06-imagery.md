# Imagery and graphic language

## Photography principles

Hair is the hero. A CHI photograph is successful when the first thing the viewer notices is the condition of the hair: its shine, movement and texture.

| Principle | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Shine is lit, not added.** | A single large soft source and a clean highlight band along the hair. No painted-on gloss in retouching. |
| **Movement.** | Hair mid-swing, mid-brush, falling from a tool. Avoid stiff, lacquered stills unless hold is the story. |
| **Every texture.** | Straight, wavy, curly and coily hair across a range of ages and skin tones, in every campaign, each styled by someone who specialises in it. |
| **Real results.** | Flyaways are tidied, not erased. Skin keeps its texture. Hair is never extended, thickened or recoloured in post without saying so. |
| **Red, black, white.** | Wardrobe, set and props stay inside the palette or neutral. One red element per frame is plenty. |

## The four image types

1. **Hair portrait.** Head and shoulders, hair fills at least half the frame, plain white, `surface-raised` or black ground. The face may be turned or cropped; the hair is the subject.
2. **Product still.** Straight-on or three-quarter, on white or `surface-raised`, soft contact shadow, label square to camera and fully legible. Groups are lined up on one baseline.
3. **In use.** Hands, tool and hair in a salon or at a mirror. Show correct technique: sectioned hair, the right distance, protection applied first. A stylist should be able to learn from the picture.
4. **Ingredient and texture.** Macro shots of silk, oil, foam or cream on a `silk` or white ground for care stories.

## Composition

- Crop square or 4:5 for social and product tiles, 16:9 or 3:1 for banners.
- Corners stay square (`radius-none`). No rounded image masks, circles or blobs.
- Leave a calm area for the logo and headline; do not put type over hair highlights.
- Text over photography needs a solid panel in `chi-red`, `chi-black` or white, set flush to an edge. No gradient scrims.

## Tools and heat

- Show plates clean and closed on hair, or open and off the hair. Never show a hot tool touching skin or resting on a surface.
- Temperatures on screens and dials in a photograph are real settings appropriate to the hair shown.
- Whenever a tool is the subject, heat protection is in the frame or in the caption.

## Before and after

- Same model, same light, same crop, same day.
- State the products and tools used and the time taken.
- No retouching differences between the two frames.

## Graphic language

The brand's shapes come from the wordmark: solid rectangles, hard edges, strong vertical bars.

- **Panels.** Flat rectangles in `chi-red`, `chi-black`, white or `silk`, set flush to the layout edges or to each other.
- **The bar.** A solid vertical bar in the proportion of the logo's I, about one wide to three tall, used singly as a divider or in a short row of three to five as a nod to ceramic plates. Never more, and never as thin stripes.
- **Rules.** 1px `line` rules to divide content. No decorative flourishes.
- **No gradients, no shadows, no glows.** Heat is shown by the stepped heat scale, never by a blur of orange.

## Iconography

- Line icons with a 2px stroke, square caps and square joins, drawn on a 24px grid, in `ink` or `on-brand`.
- No icon set has been supplied with the brand assets. Until one is commissioned, use Material Symbols Sharp at weight 400, whose square terminals match the wordmark. Treat this as a stand-in.
- Icons support a label; they do not replace it. The exceptions are search, bag, account and close.
- Claims such as vegan, cruelty-free and gluten-free are shown as text badges (see **ClaimBadge**). Third-party certification logos are used only where the certification is held, at the certifier's own specification.
- No emoji as icons.

## Motion

- Cuts and straight wipes along one axis, 200 to 300ms, ease-out. A panel slides in like a plate closing.
- The logo does not animate letter by letter, bounce, or spin. It appears whole.
- Respect reduced-motion settings: replace movement with a simple fade.
