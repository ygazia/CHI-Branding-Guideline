# Brand foundation

## Where CHI comes from

Farouk Shami was a working hairdresser who developed an allergy to the ammonia in hair colour. Rather than leave the profession, he formulated an ammonia-free colour and, in 1986, founded Farouk Systems in Houston, Texas. The CHI and BioSilk brands went on to introduce silk and ceramic technology to professional haircare.

That origin sets the pattern for the brand: a problem found behind the chair, solved with science, and handed back to stylists.

## Purpose

To give every stylist, and every person who styles their own hair, tools and care that make hair healthier while making it look better.

## Brand idea

**Salon science, everyday confidence.**

Use this line to test work, not as a public tagline. A piece of CHI communication should show both halves: the professional expertise behind the product and the confidence it gives the person using it.

## Founding principles

| Principle | What it means for the brand |
|---|---|
| **Education** | CHI teaches. Every product page, pack and post leaves the reader knowing how to get a better result. |
| **Environment** | CHI chooses safer chemistry for the stylist, the client and the salon air. Say what is in the product and what is left out. |
| **Ethics** | CHI is committed to haircare made from clean ingredients that is vegan, cruelty-free and gluten-free. State these claims only where they apply to the product shown. |

## Positioning

For stylists and style-conscious people who use heat on their hair, CHI is the professional haircare and tools brand that pairs ceramic heat technology with silk-based care, so hair is protected while it is styled.

## Audiences

| Audience | What they need from CHI | How we speak to them |
|---|---|---|
| **Salon professionals** | Performance they can rely on with a client in the chair; technique and education. | Peer to peer. Technical detail is welcome. |
| **At-home stylers** | Salon results without the guesswork; reassurance about heat damage. | A stylist talking to a client. Plain steps, clear benefits. |
| **Distributors and retailers** | A brand that is easy to present correctly and sells through. | Clear, practical, rule-based. |

## Personality

- **Expert.** We know hair chemistry and heat. We show the working.
- **Confident.** Short sentences, clear recommendations. No hedging.
- **Warm.** We are on the reader's side. We never shame hair, texture or habits.
- **Direct.** We say what a product does, on which hair, at which temperature.

## Brand architecture

CHI is a master brand with named lines beneath it. The master mark always leads.

| Level | Role | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Master brand** | The red CHI wordmark. Appears on everything. | CHI |
| **Lines** | A range built around one technology or benefit. Shown as a light capital descriptor under the mark. | CHI Infra, CHI 44 Iron Guard, CHI Vibes, CHI Care, CHI Luxury Black Seed Oil, CHI Aloe Vera |
| **Tools** | Thermal tools named by technology or series. | CHI Lava |
| **Collaborations** | Partner-led, time-limited collections. | Barbie x CHI |
| **Sister brand** | A separate brand with its own identity. Never combined with the CHI mark in one lockup. | BioSilk |

A line never gets its own symbol or its own colour for the wordmark. It is distinguished by its descriptor, its pack colour and its photography.
