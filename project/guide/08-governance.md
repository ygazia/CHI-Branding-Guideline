# Governance

## Who approves what

| Work | Approval needed before release |
|---|---|
| Packaging, tool markings, regulatory copy | Brand, regulatory and legal |
| Advertising, campaigns, retail signage | Brand |
| New line lockups, any change to logo artwork | Brand leadership. Lockups are drawn once and issued as artwork. |
| Co-branding and collaborations | Brand and legal, with the partner's written approval |
| Social posts, email, web content using these templates | Local marketing lead |
| Distributor and salon materials | Brand, through the regional contact |

## Claims and compliance

- A claim appears only beside a product it is approved for, in a market it is approved in. This includes vegan, cruelty-free, gluten-free, sulfate-free, paraben-free and any heat-protection temperature.
- Temperatures are always given in both °F and °C.
- Volumes and weights are always given in both US customary and metric units.
- Before-and-after images follow the rules in Imagery.
- Comparative and superlative claims ("the best", "number one") need written substantiation on file.
- Ingredient lists use INCI names.

## Trademark use

- The ® stays on the logo at every size and in every medium.
- In text, mark the first or most prominent use as CHI®.
- Every piece that carries the logo closes with the trademark line in `legal`: "CHI® is a registered trademark of Farouk Systems, Inc." Confirm the exact wording with legal for each market.
- Do not register domains, social handles or business names that contain CHI without written permission.

## Accessibility

- Text meets 4.5:1 contrast; large text, icons and control borders meet 3:1. The token pairings in Colour already do.
- Colour never carries meaning alone. Heat bands have temperature labels; status messages have words and icons.
- Images have alternative text that describes the hair result, not just "model with product".
- Video has captions. Motion respects reduced-motion settings.
- Body copy is never smaller than 16px on screens.

## File naming

`chi_[line]_[item]_[variant]_[market]_[yyyymmdd].[ext]`

For example: `chi_infra_shampoo-12oz_front_us_20261113.pdf`, `chi_mark_red.svg`.

Lower case, hyphens inside a part, underscores between parts, no spaces.

## File formats

| Use | Format |
|---|---|
| Logo, print | Vector PDF or EPS from the master artwork |
| Logo, screen | SVG; PNG at 2x only where SVG is not supported |
| Photography, print | TIFF or high-quality JPEG, 300 ppi at final size, with the approved colour profile |
| Photography, screen | JPEG or WebP, sRGB |
| Documents | PDF with fonts embedded |

## Pre-release checklist

- [ ] Logo is supplied artwork, correct version for the ground, ® present.
- [ ] 1X clear space on every side; at or above minimum size.
- [ ] Red covers about 15% of the piece, or the piece is a deliberate red panel.
- [ ] All type is Archivo, or the approved fallback or Noto family for the script.
- [ ] One heavy headline; descriptors in light capitals.
- [ ] Corners are square. No gradients, shadows or glows.
- [ ] Hair is the hero of every photograph, and results are real.
- [ ] Every claim is approved for this product and market.
- [ ] Temperatures and sizes are in both unit systems.
- [ ] Contrast and alternative text checked.
- [ ] Trademark line present. Approval recorded.
