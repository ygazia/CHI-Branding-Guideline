The approved versions of the CHI mark and the CHI Infra line lockup, each on the ground it is made for.

**Use**
- Pick the version by ground: red on white, white on `chi-red`, white on `chi-black`. Line lockups follow the same order, with the reversed lockup (red mark, white descriptor) on black.
- The grounds are fixed colours. They do not change with the theme, because the logo colours do not.

**You provide**
- The ground colour, and enough room for 1X clear space on every side.

**Do not**
- Use the colour lockup on a dark ground, or the red mark on anything but white, `surface-raised` or `silk`.
