The business card: a white front with the red mark and contact details, and a solid CHI Red back with the white mark.

**Use**
- 85 x 55 mm, or 3.5 x 2 in for North America. Uncoated bright white stock, 350 gsm or heavier; the back prints as a flood of `chi-red`.
- Front: red mark top left at 30% of the card width; name in bold 9pt; title and contact lines in regular 7pt, `chi-black`.
- Back: white mark centred at 44% of the card width.
- Margins are 6 mm on every side.

**You provide**
- Name, job title, email and one phone number. Nothing else: no social handles, no slogans, no QR codes.

**Do not**
- Add a second colour, round the corners, or move the mark to the centre of the front.
