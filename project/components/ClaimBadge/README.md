A small text label for product claims and categories, set in `descriptor-s` capitals inside a square 1px `ink` border.

**Use**
- On product cards, pack fronts and product pages. Up to three per product; pick the ones that matter most for that formula.
- The solid version (`ink` fill, `surface` text) is for one category label per card, such as "Salon professional" or "New".

**You provide**
- The claim text, at most five words. Temperatures in both °F and °C.
- Confirmation that the claim is approved for that product in that market.

**Do not**
- Colour badges red, add icons or ticks, or use a badge for a claim the product does not hold.
