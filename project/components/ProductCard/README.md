A product tile for shop grids: image, line descriptor, product name, size, up to three claim badges and one action.

**Use**
- Tile ground is `surface-raised`; the image well is `surface-sunken` at 4:5 with the product shot straight-on.
- Order of text: line name in `descriptor-s`, product name in `title`, benefit and size in `caption` with `ink-muted`.
- One primary button per card. In a dense grid, drop the button and make the whole card the link.

**You provide**
- A product still on white or ceramic, the line name, the product name, the size in both unit systems, and approved claims.

**Do not**
- Crop the product, add sale starbursts, or round the corners of the card or image.
