Three square social templates: a flat red campaign tile, a photograph with a flush type panel, and the profile image.

**Use**
- **Flat tile:** `chi-red` or `chi-black` ground, a `descriptor-s` eyebrow, one `display-l` line in white, the white mark bottom left.
- **Photo tile:** a hair portrait with a solid `chi-black`, `chi-red` or white panel set flush to two edges. Type sits in the panel, never on the hair.
- **Profile image:** white mark centred on `chi-red`. Keep the mark inside the dashed circle so the platform crop clears it.
- Two photo tiles to every flat tile in a grid.

**You provide**
- A headline of seven words or fewer, an eyebrow naming the line or series, and a photograph that follows the Imagery rules.

**Do not**
- Put more than one message on a tile, use gradient scrims, or add the logo to a photo tile that already has one in shot.
