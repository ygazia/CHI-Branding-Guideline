The share of each colour in a typical CHI layout, pack or screen: 55% white and ceramic, 25% black, 15% red, 5% accent.

**Use**
- Squint at a finished piece and compare it with the bar. If red is more than about a sixth of the area, either pull it back or commit to a full red panel with everything else in white.
- The 5% accent is `silk` for care stories or the heat scale for temperature. One accent family per piece.

**You provide**
- Nothing. This is a reference, not a building block.
